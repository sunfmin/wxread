wxread group leave: leaves one of the account's groups, the way a person
does, in WeChat's own window. It cannot be undone: to rejoin, a member of
the group must invite the account back. Without --yes it says what it would
do and changes nothing.

WHEN TO RUN IT
  To leave a group the account no longer wants, found by reading it with
  wxread (an ad group: `wxread group <id>`, `wxread messages --chat <id>`).
  Run by the person whose Mac it is, or by an agent they told to leave that
  group: first `wxread group leave <id>` to see what it would do, then
  `wxread group leave <id> --yes`.

WHAT IT READS AND DOES
  - The group, out of contact/contact.db (its display name, its own name,
    its members) and its newest message, out of the message databases,
    with their keys: the file --keys names, else WXREAD_KEYS. It refuses
    a group the account is not in, and a group another group the account
    is in is shown the same as: nothing in WeChat's window tells two such
    groups apart.
  - What stops it before the window is touched, answered as blockers:
    WeChat not running, or logged in as another account (told by which
    account's databases the running WeChat has open), and either
    permission below missing.
  - With --yes only, WeChat's window, for about ten seconds, once the
    keyboard and mouse have been left alone for 3 s (waited for at most
    a minute): WeChat is brought to the front, its search given the
    group's name through the pasteboard (put back right after), the chat
    it lists by that name opened, its title checked to read the name and
    the member count, its chat info (···) opened and its Group Name
    checked, Leave clicked, and OK clicked once the confirmation quotes
    the name. Each step is checked on a screenshot of the window, by the
    text macOS recognizes in it; the screenshot is kept only while it is
    read, in a directory only you can read. The app that was in front
    gets it back. Clear Chat History is left unticked: the chat and its
    messages stay.
  - Then the databases, once a second for at most 30 s, until they no
    longer list the account among the group's members: only then does it
    answer that it left.

WHAT MACOS MUST ALLOW
  The app running wxread (a terminal, an agent's host) needs, in System
  Settings › Privacy & Security:
  - Accessibility, to press WeChat's menus and send its keys and clicks
  - Screen & System Audio Recording (Screen Recording before macOS 15),
    to take the screenshot of WeChat's window; quit and reopen that app
    once it is allowed
  `wxread doctor` says whether each is allowed.

USAGE
  wxread group leave <id> [--account <id>] [--yes]

  <id>              the group's id, <n>@chatroom, as `wxread contacts`
                    answers it
  --account <id>    the account to read, as `wxread doctor` names it;
                    with only one logged in, that one
  --yes             leave it; without it, nothing is changed

WHAT IT ANSWERS
    account       the account read: its directory's name, as doctor's id
    group         {id, display_name, name, members, last_message}: the
                  group as it was read before leaving it; members is how
                  many it had, the account among them; last_message its
                  newest message as `wxread messages` answers it, null
                  for none
    applied       true when this run left the group; false without --yes
    confirmed_in  the seconds after OK the databases took to no longer
                  list the account among its members; null without --yes
    plan          the steps it takes, in order
    blockers      [line]: what stops --yes before the window is touched,
                  each with its fix; [] when nothing does
    next          what to do now

TRIPS YOU UP
  - Without --yes nothing changes: read plan and blockers, then add --yes.
  - Anything in the window that does not read as expected stops it where
    it is, naming the screen it stopped on and what it read there, with
    nothing clicked after it; a confirmation already up is cancelled.
    Running it again with the Mac left alone usually gets past a screen
    that was still drawing; WeChat itself can always leave the group.
  - The person at the Mac comes first: it waits for the keyboard and mouse
    to be left alone, and typing while it runs can stop it.
  - Opening the group's chat marks its messages read, as a person opening
    it would; the chat is left open after.
  - WeChat whose screens read otherwise than 4.1.15's (another version, a
    new layout) is refused at the step that no longer matches.
  - A group with no name of its own is searched for by the names WeChat
    shows it by; a group whose display name another group the account is
    in shares is refused: give one of them a remark in WeChat first.
  - An answer that the leave is not confirmed means OK was clicked but the
    databases still list the account after 30 s: read `wxread group <id>`
    again, and look at WeChat's window.
