wxread group leave: macOS only.

WHY
  Leaving a group is done by driving WeChat for Mac's own window: opening
  the group, pressing its menu and confirming. This build does not drive
  WeChat for Windows' window, so it refuses rather than guessing.

WHAT STILL WORKS
  Reading a group: `wxread group <id>` answers its notice, owner and
  members. Nothing in this build leaves a group or changes WeChat's files.
