# A favorite is answered as its kind, and its files are named by its items

`wxread favorites` reads `favorite/favorite.db`, where WeChat for Mac 4.x keeps each favorite as one row of `fav_db_item`: its `type` what it is, its `update_time` when it was saved or last edited, its `content` a `<favitem>` holding its text or link, its `<source>` and, in a `<datalist>`, each item it is made of. As with messages (0004), a favorite is answered as one of a closed set of kinds (text, link, image, video, voice, file, location, music, chat_history, note, mini_program, channels, unknown), its kind's fields in an object named by the kind, beside its type and content as WeChat keeps them. Its kind is told by its type, so a favorite whose XML does not read is still its kind, its fields null; a type wxread does not know is `unknown`, never dropped and never an error. A chat history and a note are their items, each as its kind by its `datatype`, a chat history saved inside one with its own items. The types and their XML are as an account's own favorites showed them, every one of a real account's 1,270 read as a known kind. Reading them is the pure crate `wxread-favorites`, checked by Flux (0002) and tested on content alone.

A favorite's file lies under the account's directory at `business/favorite/data/<h[..2]>/<h>`, its thumbnail at `business/favorite/thumb/<h[..2]>/<h>`, `h` the MD5 of the item's `dataid`, its `fullmd5` (`thumbfullmd5`) and `_data` (`_thumb`): the rule that named 819 of the 820 files of that account's favorites, the last one a thumbnail whose item names no `thumbfullmd5`, which the same rule names with it empty. A mid-size copy of an image lies beside them under `business/favorite/mid`, named with `_mid`, and is not read: it is there when an image's file is. Every one of them is sealed as an image is (V2 under the account's image key), a note's HTML body as much as an image, so `wxread file get` unseals a favorite's file whatever it is, checked only by its padding, and writes one WeChat did not seal as it is. The account's image key is found from a message's image, else from a favorite's thumbnail. A file is named by its item, `fav:<favorite>:<item>`, since a favorite (a chat history, a note) can hold several; `wxread file get` takes that id beside a message's, and answers one WeChat does not hold as missing, with its thumbnail when it holds one, as 0005 does.

Favorites are listed newest first by `update_time`, then `local_id`, so a `next` names one place; a note edited since moves to the top.

## Consequences

- A favorite's source is read from its `<source>`: in a group `fromusr` is the group and `realchatname` the member who said it; in a direct chat `fromusr` said it, to `tousr`. The row's own columns of the same are read only when the content does not.
- `file_present` and `thumbnail_present` are looked for only for the favorites on the page answered.
- WeChat downloads a favorite's file only when it is opened in its Favorites, so most are missing; the fix says to open that favorite.
- A note's body is HTML without a header, so its content type is `application/octet-stream`; its paragraphs are its text items in the listing.
- WeChat may hold the key of `favorite/favorite.db` only once its Favorites has been opened, so keys without it are an error saying to open Favorites and read the keys again. An account with no `favorite/favorite.db` has no favorites, not an error.
